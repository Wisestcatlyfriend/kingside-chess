# Kingside

Free 3D chess for Windows and Mac. Seventeen computer opponents from 250 to 3000, online games with your friends, 112 narrated lessons and 440 puzzles, all on one walnut board.

## What's in it

- **Seventeen opponents**, each with a character and a rating, from Pip (250) to full-strength Stockfish (3000).
- **Live coaching**: a coach grades your moves, warns you about threats and explains mistakes out loud. Hints and takebacks when you want them.
- **Kingside Online**: make an account with a username and password, add friends, invite them by name, code or link, and play with real clocks, chat and rematches. Windows and Mac play together. Ratings use Glicko-2 and the server checks every move.
- **Lessons**: 112 lessons in nine tracks, from how the pieces move to classic games.
- **Puzzles**: rated, sprint, daily and by theme. Every finished game gets a full review.

## Installing

**Windows.** Run the installer. Kingside isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may warn you: click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

**Mac.** Open the `.dmg` and drag Kingside into Applications. The app isn't notarized yet, so the first time, right-click Kingside in Applications and choose **Open**. If macOS still refuses, open **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**.

## Questions

- **Is it free?** Yes. No ads, no subscription, nothing to buy.
- **Do I need an account?** Only for online games. Bots, lessons, puzzles and reviews work offline.
- **Where do my games go?** Games and settings stay on your computer. An online account keeps your username, rating and online games.
