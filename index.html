Questions

Is it really free? Yes. No ads, no subscription and nothing to buy inside the game.

Do I need an account? Only to play friends on Kingside Online. The bots, lessons and puzzles work without one.

Can Mac and Windows players play each other? Yes. Both apps use the same server, ratings and invite codes.

Why does Windows warn me when I install it? Kingside isn't code-signed yet, so Windows doesn't recognise it. Click More info, then Run anyway.

How do I open it on a Mac? Drag Kingside into Applications and open it once. When macOS stops it, open System Settings, go to Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.

Which Mac do I have? Open the Apple menu and choose About This Mac. A chip named Apple M1 or later means Apple Silicon; a processor named Intel means Intel.

What does it need? Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), or macOS 11 or newer. A graphics card from the last few years keeps the 3D board smooth; there are lighter settings for older ones.

Does it work offline? Bots, lessons, puzzles and reviews do. Online games need the internet.

Where do my games go? Games and settings stay on your computer. An online account keeps your username, your rating and your online games.

Does the coach really talk? Yes, with a natural voice that runs on your computer. The first time it speaks it downloads its voice, about 90 MB.
